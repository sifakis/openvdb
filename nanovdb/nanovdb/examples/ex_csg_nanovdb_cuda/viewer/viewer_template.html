CSG operations on NanoVDB level sets

One level set stays in place and a second one moves toward it. At every step csgUnion, csgIntersection and csgDifference of tools/cuda/CSG.cuh ran on the GPU, for each combination of negated inputs. The page draws what the results store.

How to read the pictures

  • The orange band is the narrow band: the voxels that store a value. Its darker half holds negative values, its lighter half positive ones.
  • A mask bit says "the sign here is the opposite of the background". With a positive background the bits mark the inside (purple); with a negative background the inside is everything whose bit is off (grey purple) and the bits mark the outside (grey green).
  • The shade of a bit tells the tree level that stores it: one bit per voxel in a leaf, one bit per childless slot of a lower node (a leaf-sized block), or one bit per childless slot of an upper node.
  • In the slices one pixel is one voxel, and the thin grid marks leaf boundaries (every 8 voxels) where a leaf exists.
  • Negating a level set (csgComplement) flips the sign of its values and of its background; the masks stay as they are.
